# Exploratory tick-reading consensus

This crop-level diagnostic combined agreement between standard and zero-padding
numeric readings, a literal primary, a local axis-ink check, and the crop's
distance from the axis relative to other numeric proposals in the same image.
The rule and its position margin were chosen after inspecting development
features. It is not an independent validation or an application change.

| Exposed crop set | Reviewed numeric labels | Correct numeric labels qualifying | Those labels withheld by current crop signals |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Training boxes | 76 | 76 | 52 |
| Training-holdout matched proposals | 46 | 46 | 30 |
| Development proposals | 115 | 110 | 74 |

All 237 labelled standard primaries had the expected numeric value. The current
column measures only crop-level nonliteral, conflicting-alternative, and
axis-connected signals; it does not model every workflow review reason. The
candidate comparison does not prove an automatic calibration would complete.
Six training-holdout labels had no matching proposal and remain misses.

None of the 16 unmatched training proposals qualified; seven were previously
reviewed as graph structure and nine remain unclassified. On development
images, no other-text, graph-structure, or uncertain crop qualified. Four
clipped numeric-text crops without verified transcriptions did qualify. Their
values remain unscored and cannot be counted as correct. The 15 development
images containing proposals came from five studies with repeated layouts, and
the assistant's labels were incomplete and not independently adjudicated.

The result shows a direction for further investigation, not permission to
ignore numeric alternatives. No labelled crop had an incorrect parsed standard
primary, so this sample cannot measure how often the proposed signal would
accept an incorrect value. The proposals were generated by an exploratory
tick-aligned rule rather than the complete production detector, and axis
selection and calibration still need review. No production safeguard, model
approval, or release gate was changed.
